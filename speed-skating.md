Speed skating is a big part of my life. I enjoy the feeling of moving fast on the ice and learning how to skate more smoothly and efficiently. Every practice gives me something new to work on, such as my starts, turns, technique, or race strategy.

I train and compete with Bay State Speedskating Club. On this page, I will share my competitions, personal-best times, training experiences, and progress toward new goals.
