On May 10, 2025, I gave my first solo piano recital. I was very excited to play for my family, teachers, and friends.

In the first half, I played all three movements of Mozart's Sonata No. 10 in C major, K. 330. After intermission, I played Chopin's Mazurka, Op. 24, No. 2, and the first movement of Mozart's Piano Concerto No. 8 in C major, K. 246. My teacher, Ms. Jia Shi, played the piano accompaniment for the concerto. My little sister also performed during intermission.

After the concert, we celebrated my eighth birthday together. It was a very special day for me.
